same family — so a course cut from them sounds like one course rather than
seven.

## How a lesson gets one

`"music": "auto"` in a job file. The track is chosen from the lesson's NUMBER,
so `101.1`, `101.2`, `101.3` walk along the list and come back round after
seven — and re-rendering `101.1` next month gives it the same bed it had. A
random pick would have quietly drifted half the course onto different music
than the half already signed off.

By hand: Cast → Music → Library.

## Two things that are not optional

**WAV, 16-bit PCM.** Not taste. The analysis that lets a loop REPEAT reads
16-bit PCM, and without it the composition has no length to count against — so
it plays the file once from the top and leaves the rest of the lesson silent.
A twelve-second bed under a five-minute lesson would be silent for four and a
half minutes, and nobody notices until the lesson is watched to the end.

**Volume 0.12, not 0.28.** The old default was chosen when music was something
added deliberately to one video. A bed under 72 lessons is a different job: felt
and not listened to. The duck takes it further down again under speech.
